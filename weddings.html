Common Questions

Wedding Planning FAQs

How far in advance should I book? +
We recommend booking at least 6–12 months in advance, especially for peak wedding season (October–February). However, we do accommodate shorter timelines depending on availability.
Do you work with specific venues? +
We have long-standing relationships with the finest venues across Pune and Pan India — including Radisson Blu and Fairfield by Marriott — but we are happy to work with any venue you choose.
Can you manage multi-day wedding functions? +
Absolutely. We manage complete multi-function weddings including Engagement, Mehendi, Haldi, Sangeet, Wedding Ceremony, and Reception — each with dedicated coordination and themed setups.
What is your minimum budget requirement? +
Our packages are tailored to your vision and scale. We work across a wide range of budgets. The best way to understand pricing is through a free consultation with Sarika Shah herself.
Do you provide catering services? +
We work with an exclusive network of caterers across cuisines and dietary preferences. We coordinate, manage, and ensure quality — from welcome drinks to midnight dessert stations.
Can we customise the styling entirely? +
Everything we do is bespoke. We create your design concept from scratch based on your personal aesthetic, cultural preferences, colour palette, and vision — no templates, ever.
