Maharashtrian

The Sacred Marathi Wedding

The Maharashtrian wedding is a beautiful blend of simplicity and spiritual depth. From the auspicious Sakhar Puda (engagement) to the Antarpat ceremony and the Saptapadi, every ritual carries profound meaning rooted in the Marathi culture of Maharashtra.

Our team ensures every custom is observed with perfect timing and reverence — the traditional Mangalagauripuja, the exchange of varamala, and the vibrant naad of traditional instruments are all curated with precision.

Key Ceremonies We Manage

Sakhar Puda Haldi Ceremony Antarpat Saptapadi Mangalashtak Reception

Gujarati

The Vibrant Gujarati Celebration

Gujarati weddings are a spectacular riot of colour, music, and dance. Mandvani, the Garba nights, the Jaimala exchange — these are experiences that guests remember for decades. The décor is typically bold, the colours vivid, and the energy infectious.

Dream Event specialises in curating the authentic Gujarati experience: from the Pithi (turmeric) ceremony to the Garba choreography and the elaborate four-day wedding schedule.

Punjabi

The Grand Punjabi Baraat

Nothing matches the grandeur and sheer exuberance of a Punjabi wedding. The dhol, the baraat procession, the bhangra performances — it's a celebration that feels larger than life, and that's exactly how it should be.

We manage everything from the Roka and Anand Karaj ceremony to the Pheras and the dhol-lit baraat entrance — ensuring the energy never drops and the logistics are invisible.

Christian

The Timeless Church Wedding

The Christian wedding carries a classic elegance — white florals, the church aisle, the exchange of vows, and the joy of a reception that brings two families together. Whether a cathedral ceremony or an intimate chapel, we handle every detail with grace.

From the floral arch at the entrance to the reception hall décor, table arrangements, live music, and the wedding cake — Dream Event crafts Christian weddings that are simply breathtaking.

South Indian

The Sacred South Indian Ceremony

South Indian weddings — whether Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, or Malayali — are deeply rooted in tradition and Vedic rituals. The vibrant floral décor, banana leaf settings, nadaswaram music, and elaborate kolam patterns are central to the experience.

Our team works with expert priests and traditional vendors to ensure every ritual from Nichayathartham to the Saptapadi is performed with complete authenticity and cultural respect.

Jain

The Pure Jain Wedding

Jain weddings are a celebration of simplicity, purity, and spiritual devotion. The ceremonies follow strict Jain customs — often without a fire ritual — and the décor reflects a refined, elegant aesthetic that respects the values of non-violence and minimalism.

Dream Event works with expert Jain priests and sourcers familiar with traditional customs, ensuring every element from the Tilak to the Kansar ceremony is appropriately observed and beautifully presented.
