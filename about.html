Our Story

Where Every Emotion Becomes a Celebration

Founded in 2016 in Pune by Sarika Shah, Dream Event began with weddings and has grown into one of Maharashtra's most trusted event management companies, with five branches across Pune and a Pan India footprint.

"We don't just plan events — we craft memories that live in the hearts of families for generations."

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Every decision we make — from the supplier we choose to the flower variety we select — is guided by four unwavering principles that define who we are.

01
🎯

Professionalism

We operate with the discipline and rigour of a premium service organisation. Every timeline is met, every commitment honoured, every detail documented. Our clients trust us because we make reliability our non-negotiable baseline, not an aspiration.

02
💡

Innovation

The world of events evolves rapidly and we evolve faster. Whether it's a drone light show for a sangeet or an AI-curated playlist for a corporate gala, we bring fresh ideas to every brief — and we never repeat the same event twice.

03
🤝

Trustworthiness

Your celebration is one of the most significant investments you'll make. We treat it with the same seriousness. Transparent budgeting, honest timelines, and clear communication are the foundations on which every Dream Event partnership is built.

04
👥

Team Work

No extraordinary event is created by a single person. Dream Event brings together a hand-picked team of décor artists, choreographers, caterers, photographers, and logistics experts — all moving in perfect synchrony with one shared goal: your perfect day.
